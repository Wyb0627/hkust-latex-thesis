1.1     Query-aware Retrieval
This section is based on the published MGRAG paper [smoke:mgrag]. MGRAG ranks mul-
timodal evidence, builds a query-specific graph incrementally, and matches paths in a query
graph to semantically corresponding evidence subgraphs. Its retrieval pipeline uses the captions
of images for coarse-grained ranking with a text encoder. Text documents are split into overlap-
ping chunks. The ranked text and image evidence then provide the input for query-aware graph
construction.
   Graph construction proceeds through the ranked chunks until a semantic match is found or an
iteration limit is reached. A query path can correspond to several facts in the evidence graph, so
matching operates on semantic representations of paths and subgraphs. The generator receives
the matched graph sequence, the multimodal chunks used during graph construction, and the
query. Figure ?? reproduces the original overview without changing any graphical elements.
