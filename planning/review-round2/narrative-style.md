## Overall assessment and contribution mapping

The central argument is coherent: explicit relationships determine the evidence represented, retrieved, matched, and processed by language models. KGLink → GORAG → AGRAG → MGRAG is a progression of research questions, with separate implementations and evaluation protocols. The manuscript does not invent a common end-to-end system, or say that MGRAG calls MCMI. HyperKG is separately identifiable in Background and is not promoted to a fifth language-model contribution. Preserve this organization.

The principal remaining weakness is repetition of instructions about how evidence should be read. Many individual caveats are accurate, but a technical explanation is often followed by a second paragraph explaining that the illustration, ablation, or table has its own scope. The same explanation then returns in the chapter summary and Discussion. This adds text without deepening the argument. The fix is mostly deletion and consolidation, with the material protocol condition retained beside its experiment.

One concrete mapping correction is required: Discussion Table 7.1 describes GORAG's language-model role as keyword extraction, although Chapter 4 defines statistical keyword extraction. The model generates label descriptions/representative information and performs classification.
