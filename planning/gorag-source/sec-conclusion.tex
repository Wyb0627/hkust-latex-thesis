\section{Conclusion}\label{sec:conclusion}

In this paper, we propose GORAG, a Graph-based Online Retrieval Augmented Generation framework for the Dynamic Few-shot Social media Text Classification (DFSTC) task. 
Extensive experiments on hate speech and misinformation detection benchmarks with different characteristics demonstrate the effectiveness of GORAG 

in classifying social media texts with only a limited number or even no labeled data. 
GORAG shows its effectiveness in adapting to the dynamic updates of target labels by adaptively retrieving candidate labels to filter the large target label set in each update round. 

For future work, we aim to further enhance GORAG's performance and explore its application in more general scenarios.
