                      V. C ONCLUSION
   This paper presents GERAG , a Graph-based Retrieval
Augmented Generation framework addressing the high com-
putational costs, limited reasoning capabilities, and poor gen-
eralizability on complex QA tasks. GERAG introduces a
lightweight, statistics-based NER method to replace costly
LLM-driven entity extraction, improving efficiency without
sacrificing effectiveness. By formulating retrieval as a Min-
imum Cost Maximum Influence (MCMI) subgraph generation
problem, GERAG captures query-relevant semantic dependen-
cies by providing explicit reasoning subgraph to help LLM
inference, make the framework able to generalize to complex
QA tasks. Experiments on real-world datasets demonstrate the
effectveness and efficiency of GERAG over state-of-the-art
RAG models.
