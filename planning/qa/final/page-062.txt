5.2 Framework and Graph Construction
5.2.1 The three-stage pipeline
AGRAGseparatesdatapreparation,graphretrieval,andhybridretrieval. Datapreparationbuilds
the graph and the passage indexes. Graph retrieval conditions the graph on a query through
semantic mapping and influence scores, then selects a connected evidence subgraph. Hybrid
retrieval combines dense passage similarity with BM25. Figure 5.1 reproduces the original
architecture.
