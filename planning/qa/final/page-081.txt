6.1 Multimodal Evidence and Query Requirements
Multimodal questions can require relationships distributed across text and images. A passage
may name a person, an image may show an action, and another passage may link that person
to an event. Retrieving the items independently can leave the required connection unresolved.
We construct query-related graphs and match their paths to the relationships requested by the
question.
