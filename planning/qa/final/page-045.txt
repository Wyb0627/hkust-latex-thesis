3.7 Discussion and Chapter Summary
We proposed KGLink to supply missing table context and learn the correspondence between
graph descriptions and annotation labels. Cross-column filtering selects entity candidates, re-

tables without retrieved KG evidence, the trained encoder continues to annotate from the table
values.

processing stage can use only entities and relations available to its linker and neighborhood
retrieval. The supervised predictor learns the type set represented in training. The evaluated
