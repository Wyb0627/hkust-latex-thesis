A basic passage pipeline divides a corpus into chunks, indexes them, scores the chunks for
a query, and concatenates selected text with the query. The chunk boundary establishes the
retrievable unit. Small chunks can isolate a relevant statement but split its explanation from
necessary context. Large chunks can retain more context while using a larger part of the gen-
eration budget. Overlap changes how adjacent chunks share information. These choices affect
