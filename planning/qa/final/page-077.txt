5.7.5 Component ablations
Theablation compares hybrid retrievalalone, language-modelentity extraction, graph retrieval
without hybrid context, an initial tree without MCMI expansion, and retrieval without the raw-
tripletfilteringstep.
The hybrid-only variant has Novel creative-generation faithfulness 0.491, accuracy 0.322,
and coverage 0.378. Full AGRAG reports 0.513, 0.339, and 0.386. For Novel contextual sum-
marization, hybrid-only coverage and accuracy are 0.739 and 0.522, compared with 0.778 and
0.643 for the complete system. Adding graph retrieval improves creative-generation scores and
summarizationaccuracy.
Removing hybrid retrieval produces a different pattern. Novel creative-generation faithful-
ness is 0.519, exceeding the complete system’s 0.513, while accuracy and coverage decrease to
0.306 and 0.343. Medical contextual-summarization coverage decreases from 0.690 to 0.420.
The graph-only variant can produce well-grounded claims while supplying less of the required
information.
Replacing statistical entity extraction with model-based extraction gives Novel creative-
generation faithfulness 0.428 and accuracy 0.302, with indexing time 83.3 minutes in the ab-
lation setting. The complete configuration reports 65.6 minutes in that table. This experiment
changesentityextractionwhileretainingtherestofthemethod.
The variant without MCMI expansion uses a Steiner-tree representation. Its Novel
contextual-summarization accuracy is 0.592, compared with 0.643 for full AGRAG, and its
Medical creative-generation accuracy is 0.530, compared with 0.598. Some fact-retrieval
metrics are close or favor the tree variant. Expansion improves these summarization and
creative-generationscores,whiletheinitialtreeremainscompetitiveonfactretrieval.
Removing raw-triplet filtering gives Novel contextual-summarization coverage 0.712 and
accuracy0.597. Thesearebelow0.778and0.643forfullAGRAG.Filteringthemappedtriples
improvescoverageandaccuracy.
