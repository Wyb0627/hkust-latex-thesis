The AGRAG ablations show why coverage and faithfulness must be considered together.
Without hybrid passages, the generator can produce supported claims while omitting required
information. Graph connectivity helps organize facts, while passages preserve context that ex-
tractionorsubgraphselectioncanomit.
MGRAG separates evidence retrieval from reuse of its computation. Cache comparisons
measure combined retrieval and serving choices. Inverse ablations test additions within one
retrievalpipeline,whereimprovedanswerqualitycanrequireextraruntime.
Indexing and query costs also need to remain separate. KGLink reports preprocessing and
model-processing costs for annotation. GORAG reports the resource demands of its evolving
index and retrieval. AGRAG separates corpus indexing from querying. MGRAG reports con-
struction,matching,answer-generation,andend-to-endcosts.
