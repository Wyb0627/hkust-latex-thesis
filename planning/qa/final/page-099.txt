Thepublicationstatesthatpath-basedsemanticsubgraphmatchingisNP-hard. Itsproofsketch
reduces subgraph isomorphism to a restricted instance with uniform embeddings and unit path
lengths. Theresultmotivatestheuseofsemanticpathgrowthandconstrainedmappingenumer-
ation. The complexity of enumerating mappings remains relevant even when each individual
extensionisselectedgreedily.
