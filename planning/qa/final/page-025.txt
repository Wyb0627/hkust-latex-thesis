Knowledge-based methods obtain evidence through a different route. HNN combines
learned table features with knowledge-base lookup and inter-column semantics [12]. A
knowledge resource can identify a relation between mentioned objects that the table’s strings
do not explain. RECA uses related tables as an additional contextual source [13]. These
approaches show two choices for extending the information available within the target table:
retrieving external semantic facts and identifying evidence from other tables. Their retrieval
