HippoRAG combines language-model extraction, a knowledge graph, and personalized
PageRank for retrieval [18]. Query-related entities seed a graph process whose scores depend
on connectivity. A node can therefore receive retrieval influence through paths from the initial
query representation. Personalized propagation introduces a query-dependent ranking over
the index. It is distinct from a rule requiring every returned item to connect a specified set of
terminalsinoneselectedsubgraph.
LightRAG integrates graph structures with vector representations and uses retrieval at low
and high levels [19]. Its design distinguishes more specific entity-oriented information from
broader relational or conceptual information and includes incremental index updates. These
mechanisms address the representation and maintenance of a graph retrieval source. The gran-
ularity of the retrieval result remains a separate design choice from how the source graph was
built.
