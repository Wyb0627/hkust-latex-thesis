6.11.2 Adding components to other pipelines
Table 6.8 retains the inverse ablations. Adding graph-aware KV recomputation to HippoRAG2
changes accuracy from 0.6335 to 0.6855 and time from 7.5 to 7.6 hours. Adding path-based
semantic matching gives accuracy 0.7372 and time 4.1 hours. Combining both gives accuracy
0.7442 and time 4.3 hours. The latter configuration shares MGRAG’s reported values because
thepublicationidentifiesitsretrievalandgenerationsettingwiththefullmethodoncebothcom-
ponentsareincorporated.
For VanillaRAG, the base accuracy is 0.7053 at 2.6 hours. Adding path matching gives
0.7175 at 4.7 hours, and adding graph-aware recomputation as well gives 0.7232 at 4.8 hours.
Thus,thecomponentadditionsincreasequalityinthesemeasuredcompositions,whiletheirtime
changesdependonthehostpipeline.
