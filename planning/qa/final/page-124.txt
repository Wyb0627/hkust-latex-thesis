7.5 Error Propagation Across Structural Decisions
The location of a structural decision affects how an error can propagate. An incorrect KGLink
entity link changes the descriptions offered to the encoder for a table. In GORAG, an incorrect
predicted label can become an association available to later queries. AGRAG reuses a corpus
graph, so an extracted relation can influence several query-specific selections. MGRAG con-
structs a new graph for each query, but a mistaken accepted match can end construction before
later chunks are examined. These consequences follow from the lifecycles of the four evidence
structures.
They identify different targets for feedback. Entity disambiguation concerns the link from
a cell to external knowledge. Online correction concerns an association already inserted into
an index. Relation verification concerns the connection from graph content to source passages.
Matching diagnosis concerns entity alignment, relation semantics, and the path search. Evalu-
ating feedback at those stages would connect an observed answer or classification error to the
structural decision that made the evidence available. Chapter 8 develops this direction while
retainingthesequenceinwhichfeedbackbecomesavailable.
