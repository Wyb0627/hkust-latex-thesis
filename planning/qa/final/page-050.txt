3.6.5 Type Granularity in the Reported Cases
Among SemTab classes with more than ten test samples, Athlete, Protein, and Film have the
threelargestreportedaccuracyincreaseswhentherepresentation-generationtaskisadded.
The examples distinguish relevance from exact label identity. Protein columns can receive
KGcandidatessuchasGeneorEnzymes. FilmcolumnscanreceiveTelevisionseries,Scholarly
article,orBook. Thesedescriptionscontainrelatedsemanticinformationbutaredifferentfrom

thereforeaddressesacorrespondencebetweenevidenceandtaskvocabularythatcandidate-type

The Athlete example makes the same issue visible at a broader semantic level. Graph evi-

of description. KGLink preserves that evidence as language-model input and lets supervised
annotationdeterminetheoutputcategory.
