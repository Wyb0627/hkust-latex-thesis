4.5.1 Compared methods and controls
The study compares six baselines. Entailment constructs text–label entailment pairs and fine-
tunes a classifier [36]. NaiveRAG retrieves text chunks from the indexed support information.
Retrieved context is compressed by LongLLMLingua before classification [41]. GraphRAG,
LightRAG, and HippoRAG2 supply graph-based retrieval alternatives [17, 19, 42]. The graph
retrievalsystemsareadaptedtoreturnclassificationevidence.
Entailment uses RoBERTa-large for five training epochs, a batch size of 16, a learning rate
of 10−6, and Adam. LongLLMLingua is evaluated with compression rates of 0.75, 0.8, and
