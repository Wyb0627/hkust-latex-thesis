The association of a qualifier with a statement is especially relevant when a subject partici-

fact. A model following ordinary graph paths then receives the transformed connections. The
Hyper method supplies additional connections intended to retain more of these associations,
whereas a qualifier integrator receives the main fact and its qualifier pairs together. This com-
