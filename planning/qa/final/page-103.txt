6.12 Discussion and Chapter Summary
We proposed MGRAG to construct and match multimodal evidence for individual queries.
Query-aware extraction selects relevant facts, semantic path matching connects them to the
query, and graph-aware recomputation reuses processing from construction during answer
generation.
MGRAG obtains the highest values across the seven datasets’ four answer metrics. Con-
struction and matching substitutions show the benefits of query-dependent extraction and path
semantics. The hop-group results show larger quality gains for questions requiring more rela-
tional steps, with added runtime. Cache and inverse-ablation results show how recomputation
changesqualityandcostwithindifferentretrievalpipelines.
Caption omissions and extraction errors can exclude relevant facts before matching. An
accepted match ends construction, so an incorrect correspondence can prevent examination of
laterchunks. Theconstructionandpath-lengthcapsfurtherrestrictthesearch.
