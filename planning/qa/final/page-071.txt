5.1 Research Problem and Evidence Requirements
A retrieval system can locate passages relevant to a question while still leaving the generator
with the task of discovering how those passages fit together. This distinction matters when a
question asks for a summary, an explanation, or a response that combines information from
different parts of a corpus. Similar passages may repeat one fact. Passages containing different
parts of an answer may share few words. A graph provides an explicit representation of these
connections,butthesystemmuststilldecidewhichpartofthegraphtosupplytothegenerator.
