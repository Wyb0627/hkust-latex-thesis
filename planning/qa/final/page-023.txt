2.1 Knowledge Graphs and Language Models
A knowledge graph represents entities and their relationships explicitly. In a conventional rela-
tional representation, a fact is a triple (𝑠,𝑟,𝑜), where 𝑠 is a subject entity, 𝑟 a relation, and 𝑜 an
object entity. A set of these facts forms a directed graph whose edge labels identify relations.

Atablecellorquerycontainsamention,whileanentitynodereferstoanindexedobject. Entity

Graph topology records the connections available to a computation. A neighborhood iden-
tifies adjacent entities and relations; a path composes a sequence of such connections; and a
subgraph retains a selected set of nodes and edges. These objects support different operations.
