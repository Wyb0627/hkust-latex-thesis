8.2 Future Directions
8.2.1 Feedback for structure maintenance
Future work can use feedback to maintain evidence structures. A retrieved relation may prove
irrelevant, its source passage may be insuﬀicient, or a prediction inserted into an index may
later be corrected. These observations could guide which associations are retained, revised, or
removed.
Feedback timing determines which updates are possible. An online correction becomes
available after the original prediction, and an answer assessment can guide only subsequent
retrieval. Recording this timing and the feedback source would allow correction and retention
policiestobeevaluatedunderexplicitsupervisionconditions.
