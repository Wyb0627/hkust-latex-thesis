The thesis draws on five publications. KGLink was published at ICDE 2024. Understanding
the Embedding Models on Hyper-relational Knowledge Graph was published at CIKM 2025.
GORAG was published at WWW 2026, AGRAG at ICDE 2026, and MGRAG in Volume 19
of the Proceedings of the VLDB Endowment. Table 1.2 records their roles in the thesis. Each
