8.2 Future Directions
8.2.1 Feedback for structure maintenance
A first direction is to connect evidence maintenance with feedback about its use. The current
studies select graph information from an external source, update keyword–label associations,
prepare a corpus graph, or build a query-specific graph. Feedback could indicate whether a
retrieved relationship was relevant, whether its supporting passage was suﬀicient, or whether a
prediction inserted into an index was later corrected. Such feedback would give maintenance a
moredirectconnectiontoevidencequality.
The evaluation of this direction should preserve the sequence in which feedback becomes
available. In an online classifier, a later correction differs from a gold label supplied before
prediction. In a retrieval system, the quality of one answer can affect later evidence selection
onlyafteritsassessmentisavailable. Explicitfeedbacktimingandprovenancewouldpermitthe
studyofcorrectionandretentionpolicieswithoutconflatingthemwithadditionalsupervision.
8.2.2 Multimodal structure and provenance
A second direction concerns the quality of multimodal graph representations. Caption-based
ranking provides an eﬀicient text interface for images, while query-aware extraction can tar-
get details relevant to a question. Both operations influence which visual information becomes
available to semantic matching. A useful extension would retain the link from a graph fact to
thetextspanorimageregionsupportingit,togetherwiththeextractionconditions.
This would support a more detailed analysis of retrieval errors. A failed match could result
from missing visual evidence, an extraction error, an unsuitable relation representation, or a
searchdecision. Distinguishingthesestageswouldhelpdeterminewhichcomponenttoimprove.
It would also make it possible to assess whether a matched path is supported by the source
evidenceatthegranularityusedintheanswer.
