4.5 Experimental Design
We evaluate GORAG on CAD, WOS, and IFS-Rel. CAD contains social media posts anno-
tated for contextual abuse [39]. WOS contains academic texts associated with research-area
labels [40]. IFS-Rel supplies relation-classification examples derived from news text in an in-
cremental few-shot benchmark [36]. These datasets exercise different label vocabularies and
textlengthswithinthedynamicclassificationsetting.
The four-round split introduces 13 labels across CAD’s rounds, 133 across WOS, and 64
across IFS-Rel. The testcollections contain 817, 9,396, and 2,560 examples,respectively. IFS-
Rel uses 40 test examples per label. Its balanced test allocation differs from the changing class
frequenciesintheothercollections.
